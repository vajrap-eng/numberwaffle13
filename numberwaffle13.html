How to play

Swap tiles until the board is solved, without running out of swaps.

  • Every full row and full column (the odd-numbered lines) holds each number from 1 to N exactly once.
  • Each dark circle is a clue: the two tiles its arrows point at add up to its number. It turns green when they do.
  • A tile turns green and locks once it's in its final spot (except in Fog).
  • Tap two tiles to swap them, or drag one onto the other. On a keyboard, use the arrow keys and Enter.
  • The board was scrambled with the minimum number of swaps shown under the board, so it can always be solved.

Modes: Daily (the same board for everyone today) · Classic · Giant (17×17) · Fog (tiles never lock) · Blitz (beat the clock) · Perfect (no spare swaps).
